University of Science and Technology of China

Supervisor: Prof. Yongquan Xue · Co-supervisor: Ruisong Xia (夏瑞崧)

Project / Thesis Research: DRW Model Robustness in AGN Optical Variability
  • Research question: Test how departures from a damped random walk (DRW) affect recovered variability timescales (τ) and inferred correlations between τ and black hole mass (MBH).
  • Implemented workflow: Generated mock light curves under multiple power spectral density (PSD) assumptions, fitted them with a common DRW model, and compared input and recovered timescales and scaling-relation slopes.
  • Sampling comparisons: Tested fixed sampling and cadence scaled to the input timescale; carried out a DRW control test with real-survey sampling to distinguish sampling effects from model misspecification.
  • Ongoing extension: Extending the pipeline to systematic high- and low-frequency PSD-slope scans and further tests of the robustness of physical correlations.
